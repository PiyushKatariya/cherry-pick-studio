  Added a withBusy(btn, label, fn) helper that wraps every async action. When
  you click a button it:
  - disables the button immediately (a guard if (btn.disabled) return blocks
  double-clicks/double-submission), and

  locked, in which case it stays disabled.

  the run finishes (summary/done event), then restores.

  - completeStep(stepId) marks a step .done — disables all its inputs/buttons
  and shows a small 🔒 locked badge.
